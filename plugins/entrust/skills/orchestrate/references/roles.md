# Roles

A role is what one agent is spawned to do: its responsibility, what it may write, what it returns and when it is spawned. Choose the role by the work, then the tier from the page's table: top is Fable or `gpt-6-astra`, strong is Opus or `gpt-5.6-sol`, cheap is Sonnet or `gpt-5.6-terra`, bulk is Haiku or `gpt-5.6-luna`, Luna preferred. Every agent returns the five fields; "returns" below is what its `result` carries. "May write" is the rights level of its brief: nothing means a read agent, whose only writable root is its temporary directory. The list is the roles seen so far; a task may need one it does not name, defined the same way.

No role is a phase of one piece of work. Anthropic reports that agents split by software-development phase spent more tokens on coordination than on work (2026-01), and in one controlled study the planner and critic prompts of such a team evolved to empty; the roles here are cut by tool, rights and judgement. Scouting the work-list inline, the plan, the synthesis and the landing of harvests stay the coordinator's own hands.

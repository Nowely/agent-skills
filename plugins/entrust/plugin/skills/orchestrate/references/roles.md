# Roles

A role is what one agent is spawned to do: its responsibility, what it may write, what it returns and when it is spawned. Choose the role by the work, then the tier from the page's table: top is Fable or Astra, strong is Opus or Sol, cheap is Sonnet or Terra, bulk is Haiku or Luna, Luna preferred. Every agent returns the five fields; "returns" below is what its `result` carries. "May write" is the rights level of its brief: nothing means a read agent, whose only writable root is its temporary directory. Every brief whose commands may print more than twenty lines names the runner, the page's `scripts/capture-check.mjs`, by its absolute path, and the agent quotes each run's `EXIT=` line in its `evidence`. The list is the roles seen so far; a task may need one it does not name, defined the same way.

No role is a phase of one piece of work. Anthropic reports that agents split by software-development phase spent more tokens on coordination than on work (2026-01), and in one controlled study the planner and critic prompts of such a team evolved to empty; the roles here are cut by tool, rights and judgement. Scouting the work-list inline, the plan, the synthesis and the landing of harvests stay the coordinator's own hands.

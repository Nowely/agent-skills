# 03 — the claims of 01-design checked one by one

Twenty Codex Luna agents, one per claim of `02-claims.md`, read-only with no network, effort low, run as one swarm with at most eight at once on 2026-09-28. Each opened the claim's source, found the quote with `grep -n -F` and returned one verdict from a closed set: confirmed, overstated, contradicted, absent, unknown. Tally: 15 confirmed, 5 overstated, none contradicted, absent or unknown; every run exited 0. Their 20 reports hold 1,085,267 tokens, 837,120 of them cached input. Each line below is the first line of an agent's return, as written.

## 3. The plan for the rewrite, ranked

1. **Build up from the probe, not down from the present.** Start at 7ec4da9 (about 3,000 characters). Each
   addition must name the run that showed its absence costing something. Cutting down from 115,000 leaves "stays"
   as the default, which is the ratchet. Cost: a top-tier writer, one session of writing.
2. **Add the one carrier the test found, as a concept and not a case.** A short "Invariants" list, defined once,
   in terms the model knows (single source of truth, ownership):
   - a fact is a value, a row, a case or a condition;
   - a value belongs to the owner of the repository: it changes only on their words naming the new value;
   - two places that disagree are a question for the owner, never a merge; a delegated choice keeps both.
   No mention of pilots, rows or E117. About 400 characters.
3. **Close the reader's loop.** Step 5 keeps its one question. A `does not hold` is restored or put to the user as
   a question; the session's judgement keeps none; a restored line is read again by the gate, not by a second

   so the plan's word covers it. About 200 characters. It ran in M1 on a delegated word where C2's two judges did
   not: one agent with one question was taken as proportionate. One run; level 3 for that run only.

## 4. The strongest case against this plan

- **Item 2 is the ratchet's own move.** A run showed a loss, and I answer with a sentence, fitted on the case that
  found it. "A concept, not a case" is what every earlier addition also claimed.
- **The present rnd won, and cheaper.** 54 of 54 twice at 0.42 and 0.66 against 52 at 0.85. The economical course
  is to keep the present body, which is all C1 and C2 used, delete step 9 and the pages nobody opened, and stop.
  That is one Change, not a rewrite, and it risks nothing that passed today.
- **One session a side, both ways.** The first return over-read two runs; this one may be over-reading four. M1's
  reader ran on the second ask; in another run it may be C2's judges again.
- **A held-out case that I choose "where two places differ in a value" is chosen to resemble e117.** It tests the
  class I already believe in, not what else the present text may be carrying.
- **The budgets are arbitrary**, and Create has no evidence at all: K1 shows Create sessions read every page and
  plan in full, and a 6,000-character page may lose that.
- **Sonnet only**, on a harness whose scripted owner is not the real one.

I hold the plan against these because the present body is at its ceiling with no way to say what in it may go,
and today is the first evidence of which 1,400 characters matter; a rewrite that must earn each line is the only
form in which that evidence stays usable. The second objection is the serious one, and the held-out test answers
it directly: the present rnd is an arm, and if the new text does not match it there, the fallback is exactly that
Change, cutting step 9 and the unopened pages from the present body.

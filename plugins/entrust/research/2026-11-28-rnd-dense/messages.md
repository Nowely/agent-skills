# The owner's decisions, 2026-11-28

Paraphrased by the coordinator from the owner's messages in the session and approved by the owner for this record.

1. Skills are written dense: every word, sentence and step carries weight; no caveats or hedges, the essence only;
   trust the model, even at a small cost in a run's score.
2. Skills are structured: numbered steps, one condition per bullet, tables for choices, headings that name what a
   section does; dense prose does not help.
3. Prevent the endless addition of caveats from critics and judges; keep balance.
4. Raise the abstraction where the model knows the concept (code smells, programming principles) instead of listing
   rules.
5. A concern an agent cannot hold beside its work goes to a subagent with one focused question; a line in the body
   that asks for it costs nothing and does nothing.
6. The weightier the decision, the stronger the model.
7. Standing word: lines of the old `rnd` may be retired by the rewrite unless the test on a held-out case shows that
   their absence costs.
8. Fan-outs: a plan the owner approved that names its agents with their models is the owner's word for them.

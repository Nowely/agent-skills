# 10. skill-creator v2: design, build and test

Written after the run ended. The owner's messages are paraphrased; private material (transcripts, fact lists, the
corpus, the 2a key) stays out. Times of the early decision-log rows were written from memory and ran ahead of the
clock, so this record gives order and commits. Labels such as `a1r` name sessions inside the test only.

## Question and result

**Question.** After v1 (Part A `Back to design`, Part R `Undecided`, strictly `Back to design`), can v2 close what
failed: a "nothing lost" check no page session opened, a plan that missed the decisions every arm missed, no run
record, no reflection role, and judges briefed from the coordinator's habits instead of the pages?

**Result.** v2 is built and committed on the same branch and **does not land**: the landing rule needs Part A
`Keep` and Part R `Pass`.

- Part A returned `Back to design` in both cases. One page session in each case lost a fact, and the controls
  lost as many (3 facts and 3).
- Part R returned `Back to design` by the frozen reading: the decision on how a skill's text and files are
  written is only partly stated in both page sessions, and the protocol has no partial mark. Counting a partial
  mark as met, every clause of `Pass` holds.
- The gate and the plan form did what they were built for; they did not make page sessions lose nothing.
- A pilot passed after one rework. A test of the judge briefs failed once and passed on a rerun that the audit
  found contaminated.

The owner decides what happens next; this record adds no recommendation after the fact.

## Approach and why

The first v2 design built the gate, plan form, run log and reflection role in one pass, then tested. A fresh
architecture critic returned `change approach`: 1.85 of 3.2 million planned tokens went before any session could
show that a resumed turn runs a script, and v1 had failed on a rule behind a link the next turn never opened. Its
alternative became stage 1: amend the sheet first; build only the repaired test, the gate (`check.mjs
--against`) and Change's steps written out; pilot on two sessions; build the rest if the mechanism is used.

A second critic read stage 2, designed in parallel with the pilot, and also returned `change approach`: the sheet
lacked the later messages, sources had not been read, and scoring briefs against the catalogue they were written
from measures copying. Stage 2 became two slices. Slice 2a (brief form, "do not open", part mark, the verifier's
per-line check, handling a return, close-out) was tested on outcomes; slice 2b (practice step, plan file,
reflection role) was built after. The skill's scope and name were kept; the dry run and name probe that would
decide a wider loop were not run.

## What the test cannot show

- One run, two sessions an arm: an event occurred or did not (`RESEARCH.md:89-90`).
- No page session had an agent tool, so no judge the page asks for ran; Part A measured the gate and the
  session's own judgement. The gate cannot tell that a quote still says what the lost line said.
- Under the scripted keep-what-a-reader-needs reply the controls were nearly clean; Part A could show a gain
  only where they lost, and they lost as many.
- The 2a pass is contaminated and unreplicated; the handling section and the practice step have no outcome test.
- Part R's reader cannot credit what a session adopts by pointing at the page.
- The final text was not tested. Selection (Part B), other hosts and models, and a wider loop are unmeasured.

lines R1 to R13). Its main measure is new: a skill made or changed with `rnd` and one made or changed by the host
alone are each used by fresh agents on held-out tasks, and the outputs are marked blind. By the landing rule
written before any session, v3 does not land: one clause of four holds. The owner asked for the release
regardless; this file records what the checks showed.

  where it was said.
- Judges: the roles of the architecture critic, the quality critic and the verifier were reworked from what each
  caught and missed in v1 and v2; how a check's sessions get an agent tool on this host is stated in the plan.
- A retro on a use of the skill: collected by prepare-feedback, read by the audit and evidence, entering as the
  observation of a Change, with scorecard rows per part and per judge.

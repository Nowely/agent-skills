## Change

The change adds overdue-invoice notifications to the nightly job. It introduces invoice detection, an event bus, configurable notification channels and templates, and tests for the notification infrastructure.

Sources: `brief.md`; commit `ae60fd1963bea365c67582d4aad7cc9a44d5a865`; the diff against `HEAD~1`.

Requirements:

1. **Send for unpaid invoices only after the due date, using UTC calendar dates** — task; **fails**, finding 3.
2. **Use `mailer.send(to, subject, body)` and include invoice number, amount and due date** — task; **fails**, finding 2. The mailer path itself is traced.
3. **Do not notify an invoice twice** — task; **fails**, finding 1.
4. **Continue after a failed send and retry that invoice the next night** — task; failure isolation and retry are traced, but successful sends are also retried, finding 1.
5. **Preserve the existing paid-invoice closing step** — existing job contract; traced through `src/jobs/nightly.js:21`.

## Verdict

The change reaches the mailer and isolates send failures, but sends on the due date, sends the wrong content, and repeats successful notifications. The tests do not exercise the nightly notification behavior.

Target: keep `runNightly` as the coordinator and add one overdue-invoice step that uses `repo.unpaid()`, `formatAmount`, `mailer.send`, and repository state recording successful notification. Handle each send failure within that step and cover the task through the nightly entry point. The existing UTC date conversion, paid-invoice step and per-invoice iteration survive.

Recommendation: rewrite the notification portion to that target.

1. Correct eligibility and message content, and record successful sends (findings 1–3).
2. Remove the unsupported notification framework and configuration (finding 4).
3. Add tests of the nightly requirements (finding 5).

## Findings

### 1. Successful notifications are sent again every night (behavior; traced)

`src/invoices/overdue.js:2`: every unpaid overdue invoice is emitted on every run; neither this function nor `NotificationService` records a successful send. A replay of the same night also sends again. This breaks requirement 3.

Shown by: the nightly entry at `src/jobs/nightly.js:22`, followed by `repo.unpaid()` at `src/invoices/repo.js:13-15`; `repo.update` exists at lines 17–18 but is never called by the new path.

Fix: record notification success against the invoice after the mailer accepts it, and exclude recorded invoices. Leave failed sends unrecorded. The persistence and concurrency guarantees needed for a durable exactly-once claim remain open below.

### 2. The selected template omits all invoice details (behavior; traced)

`src/notifications/templates.js:2`: the template is keyed `invoice_overdue`, while the emitted event is `invoice.overdue`. `getTemplate` therefore selects the default at line 14. The resulting subject is `Notification` and the body names the event, not the invoice number, amount or due date. This breaks requirement 2.

Shown by: `src/jobs/nightly.js:19` registers `invoice.overdue`; `src/notifications/NotificationService.js:21` passes that name to `getTemplate`.

Fix: construct the required invoice message directly, reusing `formatAmount`; if retaining templates, make the lookup key match and assert the rendered subject and body.

### 3. Invoices are notified on their due date (behavior; traced)

`src/invoices/overdue.js:3`: `<=` includes `dueDate === today`, although the task says the due date must have passed. This breaks requirement 1.

Shown by: the task’s overdue definition and the `YYYY-MM-DD` UTC date contract in `src/invoices/repo.js:1`. For equal dates the comparison is true; for a prior date it remains true.

Fix: use a strict earlier-than comparison.

### 4. A general notification framework obscures the single required mail step (necessity; traced)

`src/jobs/nightly.js:14`: the job constructs a registry, channel, template engine, service and event bus for one fixed event and one mailer. Config overrides additionally allow this required notification to be disabled or routed to an unknown channel. The bus middleware, channel registry and configurable filters have no production caller outside this new path; their tests exercise infrastructure rather than the task.

Shown by: the task names only `runNightly` and `mailer.send`; the existing adjacent job directly loops over repository records and updates them at `src/jobs/closePaid.js:2-9`. The only production registrations are at `src/jobs/nightly.js:14-19`.

Fix: call a focused overdue step directly from `runNightly`, format the one message with ordinary strings and `formatAmount`, and remove the unused framework and override. This preserves the required mail path while removing the unsupported modes.

### 5. Nightly notification requirements have no effective tests (tests; traced)

`test/notifications.test.js:87`: the enabled-event test checks only recipient and count; it passes with the wrong default message. No test invokes `runNightly` with overdue invoices.

Shown by: the tests at `test/notifications.test.js:8-113` and `test/nightly.test.js:6-10`. Regressions in the date boundary, paid filtering, content, duplicate prevention, and continuation and retry after a failed send can all pass them.

Fix: test the nightly entry with past, equal and future due dates and paid invoices; assert recipient and message details; run it twice after success; and make one send fail while another succeeds, then check the next run retries only the failed invoice.

## Questions and oddities

- `src/invoices/repo.js:5-6`: how are invoice records persisted and restored between nightly processes? A notification marker in this in-memory repository only survives while its records are retained; the storage owner can establish the durable update contract.
- `src/mail/mailer.js:1-3`: can nightly runs overlap, or can the process stop after transport acceptance but before recording success? A durable atomic claim and/or idempotent mail delivery contract would be needed to establish exactly-once behavior across those cases; the scheduler and transport owners can clarify the guarantees.
- `src/notifications/NotificationService.js:31-32`: failures are logged without an invoice identifier. Is operational diagnosis of an individual failed invoice required? The task specifies retry but no reporting contract.

# Frozen instruction drafts

`00-baseline.md` preceded the ten-round series. Each `NN-reviewed.md` is the exact instruction file used for that numbered round. The round 10 file records the exact instruction used for that review. These files are historical research data, not review instructions. `11-procedure.md` is the round 11 page, byte for byte the `SKILL.md` committed with it. `12-target.md` is the round 12 page as first committed; no review was run with it. `13-blocks.md` adds the change map to it. `14-report.md` is draft 13 with an "Interactive report" section, used once in [the report trial](../report-trial/README.md); it is not the committed page. `15-structure.md` adds the summary of the change, the units and their dependencies, and the pointer to the interactive report; no review was judged with it. `16-ideas.md` adds the recommendation under the verdict and the ideas, kept apart from the findings. `17-screens.md` adds the comparison of what the user sees with the mockup and the screen before, from pictures the reviewer was given; it was the committed `SKILL.md` in the second Terra round. `18-methods.md` was the committed page in the third Terra round and the design calibration after the second Terra round and [the reading of references](../references.md): three methods in place of rules, the entries a trigger must be one of, the contracts of every boundary with the line read, and each requirement taken as a change to judge structure, with a "Shown by" line on every finding and the report's requirements carrying their source and state; it was the committed page in the third Terra round and the design calibration. `19-rules.md` was written after the third Terra round and the design calibration; no review was run with it. `20-together.md` is the page as committed: draft 19 with a design finding shown by what must be read together, not by a count of lines; no review has been run with it.

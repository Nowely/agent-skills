## Verdict

The change does not fully implement the requested discount. It excludes customers with exactly five orders, mutates cart prices, and can use stale eligibility; these defects also affect shipping, tax, and repeated summary updates.

## Findings

### 1. Cart prices are changed and the discount compounds on updates (behavior; traced)

- **Where:** `src/checkout/total.js:15` — `item.price = round2(item.price * 0.95);`
- **What happens:** For a loyal customer without a coupon, checkout changes the caller’s cart. A subsequent summary update discounts the already-discounted price again; applying a coupon afterward starts from that reduced price. The summary also displays the modified unit price.
- **Evidence:** `src/checkout/summary.js:3-7` says the same cart is reused on updates and reads the item prices after calling `computeTotal`.
- **Fix:** Leave items unchanged. Calculate the original subtotal, then apply loyalty to the discounted amount.

### 2. Free shipping uses the discounted subtotal without a coupon (behavior; traced)

- **Where:** `src/checkout/total.js:18` — `subtotal += item.price * item.quantity;`
- **What happens:** A loyal customer with a $50 cart and no coupon has its price reduced to $47.50 before the shipping decision, so shipping is $4.90 instead of free. The returned subtotal is also $47.50 instead of the original $50.
- **Evidence:** The price is reduced at `src/checkout/total.js:14-15`; shipping uses that subtotal at `src/checkout/total.js:32`. The task requires shipping to use the subtotal before discounts.
- **Fix:** Preserve the original subtotal for both the return value and the shipping decision.

### 3. Eligibility is cached even when the customer changes (behavior; traced)

- **Where:** `src/checkout/loyalty.js:5` — `if (loyaltyCache.has(customer.id)) return loyaltyCache.get(customer.id);`
- **What happens:** Once a customer is evaluated, later changes to completed orders or staff status for the same ID are ignored. For example, a customer cached as loyal who becomes staff can receive both discounts; a customer cached below the threshold remains ineligible after completing more orders.
- **Evidence:** The cached result depends on both fields at `src/checkout/loyalty.js:6-7`; there is no invalidation in the repository.
- **Fix:** Compute eligibility from the current customer on each call and remove the cache.

### 4. Exactly five completed orders are excluded (behavior; traced)

- **Where:** `src/checkout/loyalty.js:6` — `customer.completedOrders > LOYALTY_MIN_ORDERS`
- **What happens:** A non-staff customer with exactly five completed orders receives no loyalty discount, although the task says five or more.
- **Evidence:** The constant is five at `src/checkout/loyalty.js:1`.
- **Fix:** Use `>=`.

### 5. Per-unit rounding changes the discount and tax (behavior; traced)

- **Where:** `src/checkout/total.js:15` — `item.price = round2(item.price * 0.95);`
- **What happens:** The discount is rounded per unit rather than applied to the subtotal. For 100 units at $0.03 without a coupon, the unit price rounds back to $0.03, so the customer pays tax on $3 instead of the discounted $2.85.
- **Evidence:** The changed unit price is multiplied by quantity at `src/checkout/total.js:18`; tax is calculated from the resulting amount at `src/checkout/total.js:33`.
- **Fix:** Apply 5% once to the subtotal after the coupon, retaining the existing tax calculation.

### 6. The new opt-out and result flag have no demonstrated consumer (necessity; traced)

- **Where:** `src/checkout/total.js:9` — `options = {}`; `src/checkout/total.js:34` — `loyaltyApplied`
- **What happens:** `skipLoyalty` adds a way to bypass a required discount, while `loyaltyApplied` expands the result contract. Neither is requested, and the repository has no caller using the option or consuming the flag outside tests.
- **Evidence:** The only production caller is `src/checkout/summary.js:5`; all references are in `src/checkout/total.js` and `test/total.test.js`.
- **Fix:** Remove the option and flag unless a consumer requires them.

### 7. Tests miss the boundary and interactions, and assert an incorrect subtotal (tests; traced)

- **Where:** `test/total.test.js:21-27` — the new tests use 12 orders and assert `subtotal: 95`.
- **What happens:** The suite permits the five-order off-by-one, stale eligibility, compounded discounts, incorrect free shipping, and per-unit rounding to regress. It also pins a discounted subtotal despite the shipping requirement referring to the original subtotal. No test checks loyalty after a coupon.
- **Evidence:** The only new cases are at `test/total.test.js:21-27`; the existing coupon and shipping test uses an ineligible customer at `test/total.test.js:12-14`.
- **Fix:** Test four and five orders, loyalty with a coupon, a $50 no-coupon cart, repeated calls with the same cart, changing customer eligibility, and a multi-quantity rounding case. Assert the original subtotal.

## Questions and oddities

- `src/checkout/loyalty.js:5`: Is every customer guaranteed to have a unique ID? No input schema is present in the repository. Without that guarantee, customers lacking IDs would share one cache entry; the caller contract would settle this.

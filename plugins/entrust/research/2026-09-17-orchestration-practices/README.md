# Orchestration practices: catalogue, mapping and hypotheses, 2026-09-17

The question: how can the orchestrator of `entrust:orchestrate` be a more effective manager — decompose, compose and brief, assign rights, supervise, verify, synthesise, escalate, decide when not to delegate. Reducing the orchestrator's context is not the goal. The owner's four themes: (a) a table of agent roles; (b) a productive multi-turn dialogue with one persistent agent; (c) more cheap agents; (d) a capacity model with weights instead of fixed counts. Subject: [plugins/entrust/skills/orchestrate/SKILL.md](../../plugins/entrust/skills/orchestrate/SKILL.md) and [plugins/entrust/skills/codex/SKILL.md](../../plugins/entrust/skills/codex/SKILL.md) at `dff2f0b`; every line number below is at that revision. Seven agents in six waves ([rounds.md](rounds.md)): a split critique ([c0-split-critique.md](c0-split-critique.md)), three surveys ([s1-vendor-experience.md](s1-vendor-experience.md), [s2-comparative-effects.md](s2-comparative-effects.md), [s3-framework-mechanisms.md](s3-framework-mechanisms.md)), a retrospective ([t1-retrospective-ledger.md](t1-retrospective-ledger.md)), a context measurement ([m1-context-measurement.md](m1-context-measurement.md)) and the merge ([r-synthesis.md](r-synthesis.md)).

Phase 1 produced a catalogue, a mapping and hypotheses. Only phase 3 — matched tasks, independent outcome assessment, a no-delegation comparator — measures improvement.

## The four themes

**(a) A table of agent roles — contested.** For: S2-10 (removing any Magentic-One agent −21…−39 %, roles bundled with tools), S2-14 (programmer + test-designer +8.6 / +10.5 pp), S2-07 (integrator final say +9.4 %), S1-17, S1-19, S1-20, and a catalogue in every framework (S3-01, S3-37, S3-44, S3-55, S3-62, S3-73). Against: S1-04 (role-by-phase agents spent more on coordination than work), S1-03, M3/S1-21, S1-22, S2-11 (planner and critic prompts evolved to empty), S2-12/13 (a team not separable from one agent at iso-cost), S1-18, S1-47. The controlled rows split by what a role is cut on: tool, rights and judgement roles are defended, phases of one feature are attacked. Locally twelve roles were assigned without a table (T1 §4); the split critic gave the highest-value return (T1-13); one judge verdict of six fell to a check (T1-45) and one was set aside by the owner (T1-37); the completeness critic never ran as its own agent; no incident is attributed to the absence of a table. The 16-row candidate table is in r-synthesis.md §5(a).

**(b) A persistent counterpart — untested.** The mechanism exists in eight systems (S3-10, S3-18, S3-23, S3-34, S3-40, S3-48, S3-58, S3-69). For: S1-59, S1-12 (a different-lineage partner, summoned per call), S1-31, S2-28 (no equal-cost arm). Against: S2-29 (every model −39 % over turns, a proxy), S1-29, S1-30, S1-36, S1-49, S1-58, S2-24. S1 and S2 each state that no source measured a persistent advisor against one-shot briefs. Locally: 12 `SendMessage` and 4 `RESUME:` continuations, all fix or review rounds, none an advisor; a continuation cost 1.23× a fresh first-round agent (T1 §3); one writer recovered across a session end (T1-33); the highest-value returns were one-shot (T1-13, T1-20).

**(c) More cheap agents — contested.** For: S1-11 (a cheap search agent 3× faster at the same quality), S1-16 (+90.2 %, a vendor number S2 did not audit), S3-45, S3-70, S3-76, S2-15/16/17 (cascades match strong-model quality with a learned trust decision), S1-43. Against: S2-06 (mixed teams −12.6 pp), S2-18 (off-domain routers are random), S2-12, S1-13, S1-23, S2-05. Locally Luna carried bounded one-claim work at 1/34 of a Sol agent (the Luna row above) and raised few findings; Haiku's candidate findings mostly died under refutation (T1-03); the width question — 20 Luna against 5–6 strong readers — was never run (T1-U2); Terra was never used. "More" is untested.

**(d) A capacity model with weights — untested.** Published forms: per-task effort caps (S1-14, S3-05, S3-14, S3-30), width bands (O L91-98, S3-53, S3-59, S3-63, S2-05), admission prices (S1-15), stall and round budgets (M2, S3-69, S3-71), monetary budgets (S3-35, S2-15/16), learned routing (S2-17/18), user-set width (S3-74, O L41). None prices a mixed pool; S1, S2 and S3 each say so. The owner's weights Astra 8 : Sol 5 : Terra 3 : Luna 1 against T1's tokens per agent: median Luna 1 : Astra 6.5 : Sol 33 (conventional medians; the ledger's upper-middle values give 1 : 10 : 34), mean 1 : 7 : 14. Sol is the costly tier locally because Sol ran the long write and review turns and the continuations; Astra judged once per task. Tokens are not money, Claude agents' tokens are unrecorded (T1-U3), Terra has no measurement. A model weighted by role × tier would fit the record; one weighted by tier alone contradicts it on Sol.

## Three decisions for the owner

1. **S1-39 against O L25: an apparent contradiction, not a strict one.** O L25 "you never grade your own work, a fresh agent does" forbids grading one's *own* work; S1-39 (Anthropic, a rationale) says a strong orchestrator can evaluate *subagents'* work directly, which the page already allows: O L27 keeps targeted bounded checks in the coordinator's hands, and the bounds section lets the deciding evidence come "from you under the redirect rule". The record weighs for L25 on self-grading (T1-08, T1-12: the coordinator's own claims cut by agents; T1-49: a coordinator's inference published unchecked) and for inline checks of agents' work (T1-04, T1-11, T1-30, T1-31). The merge first called this contradiction strict; the coordinator's re-reading is recorded in [rounds.md](rounds.md). Recommendation: keep L25; what changes in verification is shortlist 1 and 4, since in the naming round one judge verdict fell to a check no agent could run (T1-45) and another was reaffirmed and then set aside by the owner (T1-37).
2. **R12, the completeness critic.** O L120 names it; no run in the corpus spawned one (T1 §4: "never assigned anywhere"), and the two stage-6 incidents where it would have read the text before publication are T1-49 (a README asserted that returns were lost when they were intact in another directory, an inference from absence published unchecked) and T1-50 (ten regressions in one round, all qualifications added to make a sentence truer; 4 of 27 stated checks did not hold). Owner corrections at stage 6 are 8 of 27, level with stage 1. Recommendation after the owner's reading on 2026-09-17: spawn it narrowly, one Opus reader before any text a human will read (a research README, a changelog, a synthesis), never per return; this round spawned none either ([rounds.md](rounds.md)).
3. **O L68, "measured better and smarter, and four times cheaper".** The quality half has a trace (T1 §2.4: Haiku 4/50 schema losses and most candidate findings dead under refutation; Luna 20/20 located, 13/13 agreed); the price half has none — no Haiku token count is recorded (T1-U3). The owner's view on 2026-09-17: Haiku is much worse than Luna and not worth calling on its own, so the quality half stands and the price half is re-worded or re-measured; what Luna is for is mass and fallback, entered as H16–H18 under The ruler.

R10, the pool numbers, is resolved: the owner's 2026-09-12 statement (1 Astra, 1 Fable, 2 Opus, 2 Sol, 10 Luna, 5 Terra) was a per-moment ceiling offered for research waves, not a change of the page's default, so O L41 "six alive" is current — evidence level 2, the coordinator's own note from that day, not a trace.

## The ruler

What phase 3 measures, per matched task: unique coordinator incidents per comparable run, by stage, with severity; owner corrections; independently verified outcomes, judged cross-family or by a human (S2-37); agents and paid turns under iso-cost accounting, not equal rollouts (S2-48). Every hypothesis has a comparator; where the evidence calls for it the comparator is a single agent or no delegation (H1, H15). Cells need n ≥ 50 or a stated interval (S2-42) and paired tests (S2-44); Luna or Sonnet reader panels are simulated readers of unknown external validity (S2-45). The 15 hypotheses with their metrics are in r-synthesis.md §8. Three more from the owner's reading on 2026-09-17, not in r-synthesis.md: **H16 width** — 34 Luna against one Sol on one task, independently verified findings per token (the comparison T1-U2 lacks); **H17 quota fallback** — when the Codex quota leaves only Luna, which work still goes there and which returns to Claude, measured as incidents and re-runs under that composition; **H18 task shapes** — which closed-answer forms over a bounded input Luna carries (quote checks, closed-set classification, recognition panels), per-claim correctness by form.

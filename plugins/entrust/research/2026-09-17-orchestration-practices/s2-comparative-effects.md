# Lens S2 — comparative effects and evaluator validity

Scope: controlled comparisons, ablations, failure analyses, and measurement-validity studies relevant to an LLM-agent orchestrator. This is a catalogue of bounded source claims, not a finding that any practice improves management in the target coding assistant. Stage is the row's primary stage only. “More Agents Is All You Need” is explicitly treated as sampling plus voting, not orchestration. Multi-turn human–assistant results are explicitly treated as a proxy, not direct evidence for a persistent counterpart agent.

All 48 counted rows below use sources fetched in this run and quotes present in the fetched text. “Cost” is reported only when the source gives one; otherwise it is unknown. Dates/versions are the arXiv versions fetched on 2026-09-17.

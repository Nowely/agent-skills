Design fidelity

Both accepted implementations met the study's functional and responsive rubric. Neither reproduced every design detail. At 280 CSS px the design is 499 px tall, accepted v4 is 464 px and accepted v5 is 492 px. At this width v5 is closer in total height, but its price text still starts about 21 image pixels above the design and its availability text about 24 px above. At 486 px v5 matches the design height. At 601 px the accepted v4 is closer in height. Inspect text scale, insets, vertical rhythm, icons and action placement as well as overall size.

Worker time to acceptance: v4 took 54:31 and two returns; updated v5 took 1:17:56 and three returns.

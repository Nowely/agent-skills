## V6 source review

One Terra checked the five study records and resource accounting. An independent Astra read the frozen initial v6 and compared it with the previous full texts and recorded failures. It found three actionable instruction regressions. The first example would exercise an auxiliary action on the already-selected item, where an accidental selection might do nothing visible. The resize wording allowed a worker to enter a meaningful state only after crossing the breakpoint. Finally, a single working package consumer could appear sufficient even when another affected consumer had a different obligation. The reviewed draft corrects each instruction. It also restores the update to dependent checks after a changed owner decision. The review corrected two reporting ambiguities: the narrow pilot's brief already cued part of the workflow, and identical repeat measurements referred to widths rather than timings.

After viewing the full visual comparison, the owner requested an explicit visual parity decision. Draft 09 adds matched reference/render inspection, internal landmark measurements and a per-state explanation of remaining visual discrepancies or blockers. This is a change to the candidate skill, not a retroactive change to the earlier scoring rubric.

These were findings about the text and public record. They were not observed v6 implementation failures or measured savings. [Review draft](drafts/07-v6.md) · [Revised text](drafts/08-v6-reviewed.md)

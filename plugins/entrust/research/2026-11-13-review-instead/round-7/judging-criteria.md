# Frozen criteria: semantic review quality

Assess the six reviews against the same task and original source. You see a common-field projection of the reports, not their production rendering. Do not infer a method or a quality score from report length or formatting. Raw originals are preserved by the coordinator. This assesses substantive review output, not HTML usability or exhaustive line-map presentation.

For each material claim, judge the diagnosis and remedy separately as supported, unsupported or unresolved, cite source evidence, and state material/minor impact. A design claim can be supported without a functional bug if it establishes a concrete reading/change burden and an actionable improvement. A cohesive rule group can legitimately stay together. No helper/deletion/finding-count quota.

Assess target preservation, actionability and the substance of the responsibility/rule analysis. Identify material missed problems only with a reachable scenario or concrete structural cost; do not invent new product requirements or require an unsupplied owner decision. Questions with missing evidence are not confirmed defects. Evaluate proposed mechanism removal against its independent required behavior and the actual contract.

Group reviews with substantively equivalent findings/targets, and describe genuinely useful differences without guessing arm labels. Report unsupported changes and material gaps separately from supported improvements. Preserve ambiguity as unresolved; no execution of the application or tests occurred. The coordinator will compare jointly supported results and timing/token data after both independent judgments are complete.

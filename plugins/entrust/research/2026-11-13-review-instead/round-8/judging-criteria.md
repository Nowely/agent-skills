# Round 8: separate evidence dimensions

Judge four static reviews against the supplied task, base/head source and frozen case rubric. Reviewer IDs carry no arm labels. You may inspect the rubric/reference to establish behavior, but do not require a recommendation to resemble its implementation. No project execution, new model calls, network or edits. Reviews were produced without the rubric, reference, acceptance tests or prior findings.

First assess F1 (root consistency diagnosis and complete bounded remedy), F2 (test gap), and each trap X1–X3 separately. Use hit/partial/miss for find items and triggered/not_triggered/unresolved for traps, with the review location, source evidence and a reason. The related labels/consumer symptoms are one root defect, not separate wins. Additional supported findings and unsupported recommendations remain separate. A proposal to restore current-locale behavior can legitimately touch unchanged members of the affected group. Saved historical text has a different lifetime contract; avoid evaluating syntax uniformity as a requirement.

Then assess four independent dimensions, with a concise reason and concrete evidence:
1. Mechanism identification: did the review trace the decision/data/lifetime chain relevant to its claim?
2. Necessity and cost: did it distinguish required behavior from optional convenience, reuse existing capabilities, and avoid unnecessary mechanisms? A valid keep or bounded tradeoff can be useful; listing a cost alone is not a decision.
3. Responsibility boundaries: is a proposed arrangement tied to a demonstrated reading/change burden and bounded ownership? Cohesive code may stay together. No helper quotas. If this fixture provides no demonstrated structural problem/opportunity, mark not_applicable; do not turn behavior repair into proof of decomposition skill.
4. Behavior preservation: does the recommendation preserve all public contracts, IDs/order and lifetime distinctions?

Each dimension is supported/partial/unsupported/unresolved/not_assessed/not_applicable. A single correct defect does not automatically pass other dimensions. Not_assessed is missing review evidence; not_applicable means the case does not exercise that dimension. Distinguish static support for a proposed fix from executed implementation success. No overall completeness percentage, recovery score or architecture-success score.

Group substantively equivalent reviews. Preserve missing evidence and disagreements; zero additional findings is not proof of completeness. Record unsupported destructive recommendations without averaging them into a score. Quote/refer only to relevant evidence. The coordinator intersects both judges' support and examines disagreements, rather than using a fixed success threshold. This is a small formative comparison on a case designed alongside the candidate instruction, not held-out evidence or general reliability.

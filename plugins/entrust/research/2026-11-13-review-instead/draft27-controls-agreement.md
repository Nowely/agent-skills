# Agreement on the corrected control plan

The owner asked the coordinator and Carbon to discuss four review findings and correct PR96. Two read-only
Carbon consultations used only public research documents and proposed edits, with attributed completed
receipts from `openrouter/google/gemini-4-carbon` (requested high). No private project source was supplied,
no experimental cohort ran, and no shipped skill, report script or installed plugin was changed.

## Agreed corrections

1. **Semantic evidence owns the gate.** Keyword matches and line dispositions locate claims for inspection;
   they neither prove a useful recommendation nor establish harm. Both judges must support the same new item
   and baseline retention. A material harmful recommendation holds further action for examination; disputed
   warnings remain unresolved. There is no arbitrary allowance for one harmful recommendation.
2. **Keep scope explicit.** The owner already excluded clean targets from the next round. B remains an optional
   question, not a mandatory prerequisite or a new waiver procedure. Carry clean-code safety as untested into
   later decisions. When B is chosen, compare material harm against 25 with source evidence; a reference form
   is not an oracle and a valid refactor is not harm merely because it removes lines.
3. **Name what the familiar case can prove.** `slot-edit` already motivated `instead`; C is a familiar-case
   regression/diagnostic, not held-out transfer. A small tie is inconclusive. The sole consumer may keep or drop
   `instead` for report usefulness without claiming a measured algorithmic benefit or proven uselessness.
4. **Pin actual packages.** Candidate files are `27-shown-cost.md` and `27-shown-cost-examples.md` at
   `d9bb70a49f8a959c92b89e6ea1f05d98aa4a4347`. Their bytes were verified against the commit. The base package at
   `9ee0189c365024519bae92649610aada25d4cd21` lacks `structure-examples.md`, so assembly adds it rather than
   replacing a nonexistent file. The arm label is `draft-27`.
5. **Count judging and keep controls separate.** The immediate review phase is 3+2=5 sessions; a later
   implementation/judgment phase is another 3+2=5. A1 and A2 are separate 3+2=5 questions; B is 12+4=16;
   C's full four-arm design is 12+2=14 (a preselected pair is 6+2=8). The full optional menu plus initial
   pilot is 45, or 50 with the pilot implementation phase. This is not an approved or recommended launch queue.

Carbon explicitly approved the revised proposal with no blockers. Its suggested final wording refinement,
"no upheld or unresolved material-harm warning", was included. Identifying artifact-root paths and timestamps
will be removed from judge packets, while source-relative paths and line citations remain so evidence is verifiable.
The final file also clarifies that the original plans are historical and that no new *experimental* runs started;
this does not erase the two advisory consultations.

The frozen drafts, old plans, quality-dimension definitions and round-6 scores remain unchanged. The current
launch owner is [draft27-controls.md](draft27-controls.md); the research index and PR description/comment point
there. Agreement is on a proposed method, not empirical proof that draft 27 improves review quality.

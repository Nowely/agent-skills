defects ledger; the tree was branch `codex-delegate-issues-verify`, cut from `main` at
`codex-delegate@0.14.0`. Errors each wave made are recorded beside what it produced, because a round that
only records its findings cannot be weighed.

## What the round did not settle

Nobody looked at: a controlled comparison of the bulk tier against fewer strong readers; the set on Linux;
whether a human is helped by the rule against technical fields. Looked at and unsettled: the cleanup
placeholder on a native clone-and-symlink session; two authorised credential homes; harm from the shared
temporary directory; an untrusted value reaching a rights header; a live timeout following an approval
request. The plugin's own suites were not run in this round.

## On the bulk tier

Twenty cheap seats were spent to answer twenty atomic claims. They agreed with the strong seats everywhere
the two overlapped, survived a malformed path without inventing an answer, and produced one correction no
stronger agent produced. The judge's verdict was that the tier was not useless but did not justify its
width, and that no comparison against a smaller set of strong readers was run, so the width is unproven
rather than wrong. Five or six seats would have carried the same load.

The shape that worked: one claim, one address, a mandatory verbatim quote, and a verdict of three words.
The shape that failed: asking that verdict to distinguish "the line moved" from "the path I gave you was
wrong", which is a judgement about the prompt rather than about the tree.

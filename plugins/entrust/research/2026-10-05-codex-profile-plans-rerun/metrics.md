## Blind result

The Astra judge classified A as balanced with moderately high confidence, B as speed-oriented with high confidence, and C as quality-oriented with high confidence. It found A/B less distinct because both correctly retained the required Luna/Sol pilot.

## Change from the first run

The first run had redundant short-name/model-ID pairs, an unnecessary default-effort mention, and a quality card that replaced the coordinator and split reviewers across different scopes. None of those failures appeared in this rerun.

## Limits

The sample is three plan cards for one synthetic task. It does not measure correctness, latency, savings, or quality of completed work. The judgement proves textual compliance on this sample only.

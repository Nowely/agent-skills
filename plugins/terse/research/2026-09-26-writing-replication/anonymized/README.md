# Anonymized writing research

These are public, anonymized copies of a private writing research corpus. `episodes.jsonl` has one structured record per episode; `episodes.md` is a reader-friendly table of the same 323 summaries. `principles.md` preserves the 29 principles, one candidate, mechanisms, boundaries, evidence IDs, and reported counts in paraphrase. `blind/pairs.md` contains twelve fictional comparison tasks; read it before `blind/key.json`.

Episode records retain only research identifiers, classifications, outcomes, confidence, and a summary. Direct user and draft quotations, source-turn identifiers, evidence-check internals, alternate quotations, and private paths are removed. Summaries are left in their original language and changed only where they named a person, company, project, private system, path, or quoted private material. Principle evidence passages are paraphrased, while their episode references and quantitative results are retained.

The `project` labels are stable within this copy: `this repository` means the agent-skills repository; `owner's other project A` and `B` mean the owner's two other public projects; `work project A` and `B` mean the two work projects whose source paths contained the workplace marker. The letters distinguish projects without exposing their names. The fictional names in the blind pairs remain because they are part of the test tasks, not owner data.

Raw source files remain untracked on the owner's machine. This directory contains only the public copies; it does not provide raw transcripts or the private source paths.

## 3. What all ten got wrong

**All ten account for what they keep and none for what it drops.** Every section cites the synthesis row it
rests on; no structure says which section of the genre it leaves out, at what weight, or what that costs.
Evidence, level 1:

- No structure cites s1–s3 or a fetched document. The only counts in the ten are six N-of-M figures in 07,
  copied from synthesis.md; none names a star count or a usage weight.
- Ten readings produced one inventory in ten orders. Every section in the ten is one of the current
  README's five, or one the synthesis or terms.md named (Skills table, route list, Quick start, excerpt,
  cost and files, Update, How it works); two come from a lens — 04's "What terse measures" and 05's loop.
- Two things the genre shows are absent from all ten:
  - **Troubleshooting.** 3 of 9 G carry it — superpowers "When Something Goes Wrong" (290,578★),
    code-review "Troubleshooting" (147,780★ repo-level), claude-hud (28,133★) — with 2 of 7 V and bat
    (60,555★). The surveys list it (s1:33, :122, :211, :553, :586; s2:84); synthesis.md and terms.md never
    mention it, although stages.md:216–219 records Troubleshooting among the genre's conventions (a
    2026-09-12 draft had it under an invented name). terse has the content: the audit's install-and-first-audit task reached its
    goal with nine forced guesses, "the trust and permission prompts nobody documents" among them
    (audit.md:989); the Node requirement (audit.md:872–873) and the purge of a run in the temporary folder
    (rewrite/SKILL.md:84–87) are what round 04 packed into Install and "Where it writes"
    (04-terms-rejected.md:61–62, :93–96).
  - **The owner's own exemplar.** sharpdeveye/maestro, «хорошее описание: заголовок, что это и какую
    задачу выполняет, быстрый старт, набор команд» (owner-readme-words.md:142), appears nowhere else in the
    run. Fetched: "What is Maestro?", a problem and then what it brings → "Quick Start", install and first
    commands in fences, with a "Combine Commands" chain ("# Full workflow: audit → standardize → polish") →
    command tables grouped "Analysis — read-only, generate reports" and "Fix & Improve — make targeted
    changes" → "Anti-Patterns ("Workflow Slop")". Four devices none of the ten could weigh: Quick start
    ahead of the command table; the files boundary carried by the table's grouping instead of a section;
    the pipeline as a chain of commands — another syntax; the no-slop aim carried as the rules it applies,
    not a result it promises, which is what E1 allows. Its weight is low (593★) and its banner and eight
    badges are on B's list: the owner's calibration, not a vote.

Why it is the brief's failure: the read list (structures/brief-common.md:5) gives synthesis.md, not s1–s3;
synthesis.md is a decision record keyed to the current README — 12 of its 13 TAKE rows name the current
line they replace, "Displaces … (current:N)" — so it is an edit plan for this document, not the genre's inventory; and the brief's one
evidence question (:7), "which survey rows (synthesis.md) or owner words it rests on, or say 'new'", asks
for a justification of keeps only. A writer could keep a section with no row only by calling it new, met
the exemplar only as a URL in a file of quotes, and was never asked what a drop costs.

What the brief should have said: *Your evidence is the genre as well as the decisions: read the heading
inventories in s1–s3 and the owner's exemplar (owner-readme-words.md:142); synthesis.md records what to
take into the current README, not what the genre has. For every section type that at least 2 of the 9

works, Troubleshooting, Update, Licence, Contributing — say keep or drop, and for each drop what the reader
loses and why that is cheaper than its words. A section may be kept without a synthesis row, or a
current-README section dropped without a CONTRADICTS row, if the structure says so.*

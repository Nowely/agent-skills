When you open a project, the extension writes Maestro’s skills into the repository’s agent folders. It also writes `.maestro.md`, session notes and logs in `.maestro/`, plus Zero-Defect rules where applicable.

Section: “How it works”

“Each time you open a project, the extension writes Maestro's skills into it for every supported agent.”

“Maestro writes `.maestro.md` plus session notes and logs in `.maestro/`. The extension also writes the skills into each agent's folder (`.claude/skills/`, `.cursor/skills/` and eight more), its server entry into your MCP config, and the Zero-Defect rules into `CLAUDE.md` and Cursor's or Antigravity's rules file.”

## Gaps the survey leaves on (a)

- No entry validates a cheap triage instrument against the full reader test on a representative documentation corpus, so sensitivity, specificity, false-accept cost, false-reject cost, calibration, and an abstention rule are unanswered.
- No entry establishes when “no change” is safe for an entire text, how many areas must be sampled, how recent evidence must be, or how to combine clean structural checks with unmeasured semantic risk.
- No entry compares the time and cost saved by triage with defects missed downstream, or sets a decision threshold from those costs.
- No entry shows transfer across document type, length, language, audience expertise, or risk level; the measured instruments come from news, health materials, school prose, legal text, or source-code repositories.
- No entry resolves conflicting signals, such as clean links and current fingerprints alongside poor comprehension, or high surprisal alongside correct task performance.
- No entry measures inter-rater reliability for the specific decision “leave this documentation unchanged”; PEMAT reliability concerns a different rubric and population.

## Gaps the survey leaves on (b)

- No entry compares handover formats: unified diff, split diff, changed excerpts with context, or the complete rewritten text.
- No entry measures owner acceptance, refusal, review time, error detection, confidence, or later regret for any handover format.
- No entry establishes how much unchanged context, rationale, source evidence, or measurement detail an owner needs to make a sound decision.
- No entry studies selective acceptance of individual changes, dependencies among changes, or how to show that accepting one hunk requires another.
- No entry studies ownership and trust variables: whether the owner authored the original, knows the domain, requested the rewrite, or recognizes the reviewer or model.
- No entry gives a protocol for refusing the whole rewrite while retaining valid individual corrections, or records refusal reasons in a form that improves a second handover.
- The survey measures reader outcomes and judge bias, but never tests the handover itself with real text owners.

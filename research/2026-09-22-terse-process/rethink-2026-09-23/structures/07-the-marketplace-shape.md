# 07 — The marketplace shape

> в моем понимании, главная миссия - цель плагина. Это оценка и улучшение текста (чтобы это ни значило).
> Документация, комментарии, проза, текст и даже код, и тд - не важно. Плагин итеративно через пайплайн и
> мультиагентность, храня множество правил, бестпрактис и быть способными их найти, оценивает текст,
> делает его лучше итеративно. Для этого есть пайплайн. Без признака нейрослопа, неся истинную ценность.

> As I understand it, the main mission is the plugin's goal: assessing and improving text, whatever that
> means. Documentation, comments, prose, text, even code — it does not matter which. Iteratively, through a
> pipeline and multi-agency, holding many rules and best practices and being able to find them, the plugin
> evaluates text and makes it better, iteratively. The pipeline exists for that. Without the marks of AI
> slop, carrying real value.

## Reading lens

The reader's first need is to recognize the mission and payoff before deciding whether to install.
The next need is to know which skill to run, what it returns, and how to start it.
This leaves out mechanism, implementation layout, exhaustive evidence, and warnings that belong after the adoption decision.

## Sections in order

1. **Opening mission** — Make the broad text-assessment and improvement purpose worth choosing before any mechanism; excludes technical claims, competitor comparison, and a README-only scope; **budget: 35 words; device: plain paragraphs; rests on: O1/O2, A01, A06, purpose.md:5–9, audit Reader profile.**
2. **Skills** — Let the reader choose among the three user-run skills by need and returned artifact; excludes duplicated descriptions, internal stages, and configuration catalogs; **budget: 75 words; device: three-row table (Skill | When to run it | What it returns); rests on: O5, A02, A16, current:15–36, terms headings decision, 4 of 9 G compact inventories.**
3. **Install** — Give the reader one copyable marketplace installation path; excludes alternate package-manager matrices, duplicate command forms, and opening prerequisites; **budget: 35 words; device: one `bash` code block plus one short sentence; rests on: O4, A05, A07, A21, current:40–49, 7 of 9 G fenced installs.**
4. **Update** — Provide the matching maintenance action once the installed plugin needs a newer version; excludes commit pinning, version-history detail, and source-development instructions; **budget: 20 words; device: one `bash` code block; rests on: owner-readme-words.md:105–106, A05/A08 placement logic, [MARKET] updating example; new: owner requirement not promoted by the surveys.**
5. **Quick start** — Show the first invocation and the conditional entry for a missing or badly shaped document; excludes an automatic pipeline promise and a full workflow retelling; **budget: 55 words; device: one Claude Code `text` code block and one sentence; rests on: A03, A09, O5, audit Q3, 3 of 9 G immediate first-use cases.**
6. **Workflow** — Show the two routes and the return to assessment so the skills' order is clear without repeating their internals; excludes ASCII/Mermaid diagrams and parallel narrative; **budget: 35 words; device: short numbered two-route list; rests on: A20, current:7–13, purpose.md:24–26, 2 of 9 G numbered workflows.**
7. **How it works** — Explain in compact terms that readers, rules and best practices, multiple agents, and iterative review carry the mission; excludes implementation architecture, rule inventories, and unsupported human-benefit claims; **budget: 65 words; device: short paragraphs or a four-item list; rests on: A16–A18, purpose.md:6–8, owner-readme-words.md:104, 6 of 7 J named rule/check sections.**
8. **Your text and files** — State what improvement preserves and where the generated draft and application boundary stand, without false safety assurances; excludes a permissions catalog, retention policy, and unverified absolute guarantees; **budget: 55 words; device: two short paragraphs; rests on: A12, A17–A19, audit Q2/Q6, terms decisions for “text,” “draft,” and “your files”; current:51–60 and :35–36 require correction.**
9. **Evidence** — Offer a concise, dated account of the measured pilot and a route to fuller technical detail; excludes benchmark theater, a success claim, and a standalone license line; **budget: 45 words; device: one short paragraph with a reference link; rests on: A04, A24–A25, current:62–90, audit profile trust requirement, owner verdict that the license line is noise.**

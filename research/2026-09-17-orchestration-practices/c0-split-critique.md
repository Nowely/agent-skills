## Result

Codex Astra C0: done, I critiqued the decomposition and classified every “Got wrong” entry without surveying external sources.
Verdict: accept with corrections; do not launch the briefs unchanged. The source survey is useful, but its present outputs cannot establish improved management.
What the cut lost: source genres do not ensure coverage of briefing, dependency contracts, rights, supervision/recovery, synthesis, escalation and non-delegation. Require a coverage matrix across those decisions; context reduction remains outside the objective.
Overlap and double tasks: S1/S3 duplicate Microsoft, OpenAI and Claude mechanisms; S2/S3 overlap published evaluations. Every survey combines evidence extraction with a local policy audit. S2 combines effectiveness with evaluator validity; T1 combines event reconstruction, attribution and rule mapping; R combines auditing with causal ranking. Separate these deliverables or stages without automatically adding agents.
Correct S1, keeping Opus: engineering experience and design rationales from Anthropic, Cognition and relevant Manus material. Move SDK/framework/tool documentation to S3. Exclude context-saving claims unless their evidence concerns the stated management outcomes.
Correct S2, keeping Sol: comparative effects, failure evidence and measurement validity, distinguished in its return. Retain MAST and the named research topics; add controlled agent-scaling comparisons, with “Towards a Science of Scaling Agent Systems” and “Judging LLM-as-a-Judge with MT-Bench and Chatbot Arena” as primary-source leads to verify, not sources verified here. Voting results alone do not establish orchestration benefits.
Correct S3, keeping Sol: versioned mechanisms in official documentation and source code, including the listed frameworks, CLI tools and community plugins. Give each source/claim an owner and shared identifier; cross-reference overlapping measurements instead of counting them twice. A mechanism's existence is separate from evidence of effectiveness.
Correct T1, keeping Opus: reconstruct observable decisions and their consequences. First identify exact run/session paths, IDs, date cutoff and available receipts; “three sessions after 2026-09-16” is not a reproducible corpus. Memory, pasted reports and changelog claims are leads unless supported by traces. Include successful handling and unknowns; distinguish rules in force then from current coverage.
Correct R, keeping Fable: audit quotations and evidence, deduplicate, then map and assess applicability in separate passes. Return plausible mechanisms connecting practices to incidents, with uncertainty; replace “would have prevented” rankings with hypotheses for later testing. Allow practices with no recorded local counterpart.
Survey return: one bounded claim per row, including no-benefit or harmful findings; source/version, contextual quote, mechanism, conditions, comparator, sample, outcome/cost and limitations where reported. Separate source type from study design: vendor experience can contain measurements, and “measured” does not establish quality or transferability. Surveyors should not perform the page mapping.
Mapping: the pair is 6,808 words. Reading it is feasible; repeating a semantic audit in three broad surveys invites shallow matches and unsupported absence claims. R should read both complete, frozen pages: codex is authoritative for inherited rules, with orchestrate's explicit overrides. Record file, revision, lines and scope; allow partial, conditional, not located and unknown. Unread references prevent whole-plugin absence claims.
Wording fixes: “practices … addresses” forces positive relevance; allow unknown, inapplicable and adverse effects. “Published measurements only, opinions ranked last” is inconsistent about admitting opinion; specify its role. “Contradicts” requires incompatible instructions under the same conditions, not mere differences. Treat “measured” changelog sentences as claims to examine.
Taxonomy: not exhaustive or operationally disjoint as written. Broaden 1 explicitly to split/brief/input/contract defects; distinguish composition from rights and backfill from ignored returns; add supervision/recovery coverage. Separate agent errors from coordinator decisions. Use one primary decision-stage code plus secondary tags and count each incident once: backfill or unjustified omission can otherwise overlap 3, 4 and 7. Move 5 to a contribution/cost measure.
“Wasted agent”: unchanged synthesis is insufficient—verification, eliminating an option or satisfying an independent-check requirement can be useful. Record each return's documented contribution to decisions, evidence or required coverage; flag “no documented use” when none exists. Actual marginal waste remains unknown without a counterfactual comparison.
“Unfaithful synthesis”: an unsupported material addition, changed scope/certainty/attribution, or unexplained omission of decision-relevant findings or dissent, established by tracing returns and verification into the final account. A documented evidence-based rejection or correction is not unfaithfulness.
Ruler: unique coordinator incidents per comparable run, with severity, opportunities and unknowns, plus independently verified outcomes and owner corrections. Record total agents/paid turns; cost per survivor is undefined at zero survivors. Practice counts, citations, mappings and proposed prevention are intermediate outputs, not evidence that mistakes fell.
Earlier lessons: the 20-seat wave's width was unproven, not proven harmful; this four-return fan-out plus R is likewise not shown excessive. It repeats the terse-survey ruler mistake if catalogue quality is presented as managerial improvement. The proposed outcome ruler is better aligned, but this phase does not measure it.
Read-agent boundary: accessible logs can support counts, provenance and textual mappings; read rights also permit commands and temporary-file probes. They do not supply missing transcripts, human outcomes or counterfactuals. Installed behavior, enforcement and lifecycle effects need observations; documented instructions alone cannot establish them.
Move later: adopting or editing the skill, executing changes, testing rights/lifecycle behavior where additional grants are needed, choosing optimal models/team width/escalation thresholds, and claiming causal improvement. Use matched tasks and budgets with independent outcome assessment, including a single-agent/no-delegation comparator. Drop coordinator-context reduction entirely.
